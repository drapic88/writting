## Terminology

No term enters a draft that is not in this table. Every term below is a **named move** — the book's
core device, inherited from Musashi, who names each technique so it can be referred to later.

| Term | Definition as first given | Chapter | Notes |
|------|---------------------------|---------|-------|
| The nut and the flower | The nut is the part that works; the flower is the display around it. The fault is never display — it is the inversion that ranks display above function | 1 | Musashi, Ground. Do not shorten to "substance over style", which loses the point that display has legitimate work |
| Every tool has a range | A band of engagement in which a tool dominates and outside which it is dead weight | 1 | Musashi's gun and bow, Ground |
| Choosing the timber | Matching standard of care to load: revealed pillars, inner pillars, thresholds, scaffolding | 2 | Four grades, all four used. Never a licence for sloppiness |
| Scaffolding | Code written to be burned. Correct for one case, dated in a comment, deleted after | 2 | Sub-term of choosing the timber |
| Two timings | Immediate timing belongs to the work in front of you; background timing is the tempo of the company, the codebase and your career | 2 | Musashi: "especially important to know the background timing" |
| Everyday stance | Whatever you do under pressure is whatever you were already doing, because pressure removes the ability to switch modes | 3 | Musashi says this three times; so does this book |
| Perception and sight | Sight registers what is in front of you; perception reads where it is going. Perception ranks higher | 3 | Musashi's twofold gaze |
| The living hand | Tight where it must not move, loose where the world will change. Both all-tight and all-loose are failures | 4 | Musashi's grip, finger by finger |
| Cut and slash | A cut is committed and removes the cause; a slash resolves the symptom. A slash that works is still a slash | 4 | The book's most-used pair. Extension Musashi does not make: slash on purpose, and write down that you did |
| Terrain | What is decided before the work starts and constrains it more than skill does: deploy speed, rollback, coverage, review queue | 5 | Musashi opens his fighting book with place, not technique |
| Check your retreat | Never take an action whose reversal you have not checked | 5 | Musashi: "make sure that your rear is unobstructed" |
| The three sen | Three ways to hold initiative: move first, take it inside their move, move as it moves | 6 | Keep the Japanese term once, glossed, per `../../VOICE.md` §3. Initiative is not the same as moving first |
| Holding down the pillow | Intervene at the first syllable, before a motion is committed — and selectively, suppressing useful actions rather than everything | 6 | Musashi's "at…", "ju…", "cu…" |
| Twice, never three times | Two attempts from one hypothesis is the limit; a third means the hypothesis is wrong, not the attempt | 7 | Musashi's only hard numeric rule |
| Mountains against sea | Change category of investigation, not instance. Reading → running, adding → bisecting, alone → explaining | 7 | The second half of twice-never-three-times, and useless without it |
| Renew | Change the person holding the technique rather than the technique. The walk, the sleep, the branch started over | 7 | Distinct from mountains against sea; they are a pair |
| Injure the corners | Structures fail and move at their edges: boundaries, empty sets, midnight, first request after deploy | 7 | Two readings — migration order, and where to look for failure |
| Drift | A practice diverging from its purpose, invisible from inside, measurable only by comparison | 8 | Musashi's reason for studying rival schools |
| Speed is not a quality | Speed is an artefact of being in or out of rhythm, not a property of action. The skilled never appear busy | 9 | Musashi's distance runner |
| The counterfeit | Bewilderment relabelled as depth. The test: mastery can say where its knowledge ends | 10 | Musashi opens the Void book with this, before anything positive |
