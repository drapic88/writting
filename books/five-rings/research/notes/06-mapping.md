# Mapping — Source to Derived

Quick index from Musashi's five books to where their material lands. Full readings are in
[`../notes/`](../notes/); the chapter plan is in [`../book/outline.md`](../book/outline.md).

| Musashi's book | Marks kept | Marks rejected | Derived book |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | 11 | 3 | Book One — Ground |
| Water | 13 | 2 | Book Two — Water |
| Fire | 17 | 2 | Book Three — Fire |
| Wind | 10 | 0 | Book Four — Wind |
| Void | 6 | 1 fenced | Book Five — Void |

Material does not always stay in its original book. Notable moves:

- **"Do not just read"** (Water) closes the derived Water book rather than opening it, so the reader
  meets it after the practice sections rather than before.
- **"Speed is not part of the Way"** appears in both Water (briefly) and Wind (at length). The derived
  book carries it once, in Wind, where Musashi's own treatment is fuller.
- **"Resolute acceptance of death"** (Ground) is rejected outright, but the idea people usually reach
  for it to express — commit fully once you have decided — is carried by **"Cut and Slash"** (Water),
  which is the passage that actually says it.
- **"To penetrate the depths"** (Fire) is rejected; its only transferable shadow is already carried by
  Cut and Slash.
- **"To crush"** (Fire) is rejected; its legitimate neighbour is carried by **"To know collapse."**
- **The five attitudes** (Water) are rejected as content but kept as structure: a small fixed vocabulary
  of named moves, deliberately not extended.
