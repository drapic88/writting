# Writing a story

A blog post argues a thesis. **A story reports what happened.** Same evidence rule, delivered
as events rather than exhibits.

## Read first, every time

1. [`VOICE.md`](../../../VOICE.md) — the voice.
2. [`stories/CRAFT.md`](../../../stories/CRAFT.md) — what qualifies as a story, the anatomy,
   scene versus summary, the checklist.
3. [`CONFIDENTIALITY.md`](../../../CONFIDENTIALITY.md) — **before drafting, not before
   publishing.** A story wants exactly the specifics that identify people.
4. [`CROATIAN.md`](../../../CROATIAN.md) — if the story is in Croatian. Originals, not
   translations.

## The qualifying test

Before anything else, establish all four (`stories/CRAFT.md` §1):

1. A trigger event — what happened, when, to whom.
2. A belief that turned out to be wrong.
3. A turn — the moment it broke, and the artefact that broke it.
4. A cost, as a number.

**If one is missing, say so and propose it as a blog post instead.** Do not write a story
around a gap; that is how a piece turns into a tutorial with a framing device.

## The gate

`plan.md` is written and agreed before `story.md`. `stories/_template/plan.md` is the shape.
The brief is where the four qualifiers get tested and where the confidentiality swaps get
decided.

## Workflow

1. **Interview for the facts.** Ask Dragan for the timestamps, the log lines, the numbers,
   what was actually said. A story fails on missing specifics, not on missing prose.
2. **Brief.** `stories/NN-slug/plan.md`. Stop and get agreement.
3. **Confidentiality pass on the material**, before drafting: aliases from
   `CONFIDENTIALITY.md` §1, roles instead of names, dates shifted, artefacts scrubbed.
   Decide whether the piece needs a disclosure line.
4. **Draft `story.md`** — frontmatter, then the anatomy in `stories/CRAFT.md` §2. Two scenes
   rendered in real time, everything else summarised.
5. **Hero image** via the `hero-image` skill.
6. **Check** with the `voice-check` skill, or the checklist in `stories/CRAFT.md` §9 plus
   `CONFIDENTIALITY.md` §6.

## Rules that override convenience

- **Nothing invented is presented as recorded.** No reconstructed dialogue passed off as a
  quote, no composite passed off as one person, no tidied timeline. If it was reshaped, the
  disclosure line says so.
- **No hero narrative and no villain.** If someone had to be wrong, it is me or "we".
- **No moralising close.** Name the practice that changed and stop.
- **No dramatised technical detail.** The server returned 503; it did not die quietly.
- **The numbers are real or they are absent.** Never estimate a cost into existence to
  satisfy qualifier four — go and find it, or write a field note that says what is unknown.
- Nothing gets committed unless Dragan asks for a commit.
